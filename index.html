Two-player (experimental)

Peer to peer over WebRTC, no server of ours involved. One of you hosts and reads out the room code; the other joins with it. The host's car, stage and opponents are used for both. Lockstep, so a bad connection shows up as both cars pausing together rather than as teleporting.

You pick the stage and cars above.
Your car choice is kept; everything else comes from the host.
